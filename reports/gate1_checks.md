# Gate 1 checks: position bias and visibility (dev stimuli)

Generated by `uv run python scripts/gate1_checks.py`. Intervals are 95% bootstrap over pairs.

- **Position bias** (identical pairs only): log-odds of naming the first/left image; 0 = none. `P(first)` is the share of identical-pair trials on which the first/left image was named; 0.50 = none.
- **Label bias**: log-odds toward the letter A regardless of what it names.
- **Preference spread**: SD across changed pairs of the bias-cancelled preference (log-odds). Compare it with the position bias: if the bias is many times larger, raw choices are all bias.
- **Visibility**: AUC for telling changed pairs from identical pairs on the same/different question. `says different` gives the rate of answering *different* for changed / for identical pairs (a model can say *different* to everything and still have a high AUC, if it says it more firmly for changed pairs).
- **Off-menu**: share of preference trials in which the two permitted letters hold under half the next-token probability (the local-model analogue of a refusal; the brief's limit is 0.20).
